About This Website: Serenia Kiwale

Who runs this website, where its information comes from and how we keep it accurate.

Updated 2026-11-28

Who we are

This website presents Serenia by House of Jets, a residential project at Kiwale, near Mukai Chowk, Pune. It is a marketing website that collects enquiries for the project's sales team, who will contact you about prices, offers and site visits.

Where the information comes from

  • Prices, carpet areas, home types and offers come from the developer's launch and sales material shared with the sales team.
  • Calculations, such as price per sq ft or EMI examples, are our own arithmetic on those figures and are labelled as illustrations.
  • Locality information points to live map links. We do not state distances or travel times we have not verified.
  • We do not publish reviews or testimonials that we cannot verify. Items that are not confirmed are marked as such.

Keeping it accurate

Plans, specifications, amenities and prices change with approvals, so ask the sales team for the current cost sheet before you decide, and verify the project's registration on MahaRERA.

Corrections and contact

If you spot an error, tell us through the enquiry form on the home page and we will check and correct it. Our Privacy Policy and Cookie Policy explain how we handle your details.
