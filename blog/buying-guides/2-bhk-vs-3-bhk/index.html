2 BHK vs 3 BHK

2 BHK vs 3 BHK: which is right for you

Space, budget, parking and resale compared, with real numbers from Serenia, Kiwale.

2 min read 2026-11-21Serenia by House of Jets, Kiwale
WhatsApp

Key takeaways

  • A 2 BHK suits couples, first-time buyers and rental plans; a 3 BHK suits growing families and work-from-home needs.
  • Serenia 2 BHK total area is 746 to 750 sq ft; 3 BHK is 1,036 to 1,042 sq ft (both include the balcony).
  • Parking differs: 1 covered parking with a 2 BHK, 2 with a 3 BHK.
  • Decide using household size in five years, a comfortable EMI and your vehicles.

Choosing between a 2 BHK and a 3 BHK is usually the first big decision for a home buyer. The right answer depends on your household, your budget and how long you plan to stay. Here is a practical way to decide.

Space: what you actually get

At Serenia in Kiwale, the 2 BHK homes range from 746 to 750 sq ft of total area, which includes a balcony of 72 sq ft. The real carpet area is 674 to 678 sq ft. The 3 BHK homes range from 1,036 to 1,042 sq ft in total area, with a carpet area of 956 to 962 sq ft and an 80 sq ft balcony.

Who suits a 2 BHK

  • Couples and small families who want a well-planned home without paying for unused rooms.
  • First-time buyers who want a lower loan amount and EMI.
  • Buyers planning to rent out the home, since 2 BHK units often attract steady tenant demand.

Who suits a 3 BHK

  • Families with children or parents living together who need a separate room for each.
  • Professionals who work from home and need a dedicated study or guest room.
  • Buyers planning to stay long term, because a third bedroom grows with the family.

Budget and running costs

A larger home costs more to buy, and also adds to maintenance charges, furnishing and utility bills. Work out your total monthly outgo before choosing, not only the purchase price.

Parking and practicality

Parking is often overlooked. A 3 BHK at Serenia comes with two covered parkings against one for a 2 BHK. If your household has two vehicles, that alone may decide it.

A simple way to decide

  1. List how many people will live in the home five years from now.
  2. Set a comfortable EMI, then check which package fits.
  3. Count your vehicles and match them to covered parking.
  4. Visit the show flat or study the floor plan, then compare room sizes and layouts.

If neither feels right, Serenia's C Tower also offers 2+2 and 3+3 Simplex homes for buyers who want more space. Read our guide on what a Simplex home is.
