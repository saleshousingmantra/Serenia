What is a Simplex home? 2+2 and 3+3 explained

Two levels, one home: how 2+2 and 3+3 Simplex residences work in Serenia's C Tower.

2 min read 2026-11-21Serenia by House of Jets, Kiwale
WhatsApp

Key takeaways

  • A Simplex home spreads across two connected levels inside one unit, like a house in the sky.
  • 2+2 means four bedrooms (4 BHK); 3+3 means six bedrooms (6 BHK).
  • At Serenia, Simplex options are exclusive to C Tower and available on all floors.
  • Always confirm the floor plan, the area figure quoted and the parking included.

If you are shopping for a larger home in a high-rise, you will come across the term Simplex. It sounds technical, but the idea is simple: a home that spreads across two connected levels inside the same tower.

Simplex in plain words

A Simplex residence combines two levels into one home. Instead of a single floor, you get living spaces on one level and bedrooms on the other, connected by an internal staircase. It is the closest a high-rise gets to the feel of a private house.

What 2+2 and 3+3 mean

  • 2+2 Simplex (4 BHK): two bedrooms on each of the two levels, making four bedrooms in total.
  • 3+3 Simplex (6 BHK): three bedrooms on each of the two levels, making six bedrooms in total.

At Serenia, these two formats are exclusive to C Tower, the tallest tower in Kiwale, and Simplex options are available on all floors.

Benefits of a Simplex layout

  • Privacy: sleeping areas can sit away from entertaining areas.
  • Space and presence: two levels create a larger, more distinctive home.
  • Flexibility: separate zones for family, guests, work or hobbies.
  • Exclusivity: Simplex homes are fewer in number than standard flats.

Things to check before you choose

  1. Ask for the Simplex floor plan and check how the two levels connect.
  2. Confirm which area figure is quoted: carpet or total.
  3. Check the number of covered parkings included.
  4. Think about stairs if elderly family members will live there.
