What does EOI mean when buying a new launch

Published 2026-11-21 · Serenia by House of Jets, Kiwale

New residential launches often begin with an EOI, short for Expression of Interest. If you see "EOI open" on a project, here is what it means and how to approach it sensibly.

EOI in simple terms

An Expression of Interest is an early registration. It tells the developer which home type you are interested in, and it usually gives you an early chance to choose a unit before wider sales begin. It is not the same as a final booking.

Why developers use EOI

  • To gauge demand for different home types before the formal launch.
  • To give interested buyers early access to unit selection.
  • To plan pricing, inventory and the sales process.

How the process usually works

  1. Enquire: share your details and request pricing and floor plans.
  2. Visit: see the sales gallery or site and ask your questions.
  3. Submit EOI: register your interest for a home type, such as a 2 BHK, 3 BHK or Simplex.
  4. Allotment and booking: choose a unit and complete the formal booking steps.

Questions to ask before submitting an EOI

  • Is there an EOI amount, and is it refundable? Get the terms in writing.
  • Does the EOI guarantee a particular unit, floor or price?
  • What is the project's RERA registration number, and can you verify it?
  • What is the expected possession timeline?
  • What is included in the all-inclusive package?

A note on RERA

Under RERA, registered projects publish key details such as approvals and timelines. Always verify a project's registration on the official state RERA website before making any payment.
