Living in Kiwale, Pune: a locality overview

Where Kiwale is, how far key places are, and what to expect near Mukai Chowk.

Key takeaways

  • Kiwale is in the Ravet area of Pimpri-Chinchwad, Pune, at the Pune end of the Mumbai–Pune Expressway.
  • Ravet is about 1 km away; Akurdi and Chinchwad stations are about 5 and 8 km; Pune city centre is about 23 km.
  • Schools, hospitals and daily shopping are available in Kiwale and nearby Ravet.
  • Use the Google Maps links in each guide to check live routes from Mukai Chowk.

Kiwale is a residential locality in the Ravet area of Pimpri-Chinchwad, on the western side of Pune. Mukai Chowk is one of its well-known junctions, and Serenia by House of Jets is located near it. This guide gives you a quick picture of the area before you plan a visit.

Where Kiwale is

Kiwale sits at the Pune end of the Mumbai–Pune Expressway, where the expressway meets the older Mumbai–Pune highway (NH 48 bypass). That position gives residents direct road access towards Mumbai in one direction and towards the rest of Pune in the other.
