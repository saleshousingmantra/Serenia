Serenia Kiwale Roads and transport in Kiwale, Pune

How Kiwale connects by road, bus and rail, with Google Maps links for live routes.

2 min read 2026-11-21Serenia by House of Jets, Kiwale
WhatsApp

Key takeaways

  • Kiwale is at the Pune end of the Mumbai–Pune Expressway.
  • Pune city centre is about 23 km away via the Aundh–Ravet BRTS road.
  • Dehu Road, Akurdi and Chinchwad stations are the nearest railway stations.
  • Check current status of new roads and metro links with official sources.

Good connectivity is one of the reasons Kiwale appeals to home buyers. This guide covers the main roads, buses and railway stations around Mukai Chowk.

Roads

Kiwale is at the Pune end of the Mumbai–Pune Expressway, which meets the older Mumbai–Pune highway (NH 48 bypass) here. For Pune city, public listings put the centre at about 23 km via the Aundh–Ravet BRTS road. Open Google Maps for live travel time.

Buses

Kiwale Mukai Chowk has a bus terminal serving the locality. Check PMPML for current routes and timings.

Metro

Pimpri Metro Station is about 10 km from Kiwale. Metro and BRTS projects keep evolving, so confirm what is open and what is planned with PMRDA, PCMC or Pune Metro before you rely on them.

Plan your own route

Try the route from Mukai Chowk to Pune city centre at your usual travel time. For more about the area, read the Kiwale locality overview.

How we compiled this guide: place names come from public property and business listings and are meant as a starting point. Distances and travel times change with traffic, so use the Google Maps links to check live routes from Mukai Chowk, Kiwale, and confirm details directly with each place before you decide.
