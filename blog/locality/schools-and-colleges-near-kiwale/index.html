How to choose a school

  1. Board and curriculum: confirm CBSE, ICSE, State or international board.
  2. Distance and transport: check the Directions link and ask about school bus routes from Kiwale.
  3. Fees and admission dates: these change each year, so ask the school office.
  4. Visit in person: see classrooms, playgrounds and safety arrangements.
