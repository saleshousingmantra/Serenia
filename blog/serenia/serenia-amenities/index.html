Amenities

Serenia Kiwale amenities: pool, clubhouse, podium garden and more

The amenities planned at Serenia, Kiwale, grouped by how you will use them.

2 min read 2026-11-26Serenia by House of Jets, Kiwale

Key takeaways

  • Serenia lists 18 amenities, from a landscaped podium garden to EV charging points.
  • Fitness: gymnasium, yoga and meditation zone, jogging track, indoor games room, swimming pool.
  • Family and community: clubhouse, play area, amphitheatre, party hall, co-working lounge.
  • The amenity list is indicative; confirm what will be ready at possession.

Amenities shape daily life in a gated township. Here are the amenities planned at Serenia by House of Jets, Kiwale, grouped by use. The list is indicative, and images shown on the project are artistic impressions.

Fitness and wellness

  • Swimming pool and sundeck
  • Fully equipped gymnasium
  • Yoga and meditation zone
  • Jogging and walking track
  • Indoor games room

Family and community

  • Clubhouse
  • Children's play area
  • Landscaped podium garden
  • Amphitheatre seating
  • Gazebo and senior sit-out
  • Multipurpose party hall
  • Co-working lounge

Convenience and safety

  • Grand entrance lobby
  • High-speed lifts
  • 24x7 security and CCTV
  • EV charging points
  • Power backup (common areas)
  • Rainwater harvesting

Questions to ask before you book

  1. Which amenities will be ready at possession, and which later?
