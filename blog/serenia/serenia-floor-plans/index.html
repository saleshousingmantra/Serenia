Serenia Kiwale floor plans and carpet areas: 2 BHK and 3 BHK

Unit plans by Abhikalpan Architects & Planners, with total and carpet areas for each home type.

2 min read 2026-11-26Serenia by House of Jets, Kiwale

Key takeaways

  • 2 BHK: 746, 749 and 750 sq ft total area (674 to 678 sq ft carpet).
  • 3 BHK: 1,036, 1,038 and 1,042 sq ft total area (956 to 962 sq ft carpet).
  • Total area includes the balcony; carpet area is the usable area inside the home.
  • Simplex floor plans for C Tower are shared by the relationship manager.

Serenia's unit plans are by Abhikalpan Architects & Planners, Pune. Here are the plans, the areas and what to look for before you choose.

What to look for in a plan

  1. Is the living room wide enough for your furniture, and where does the light come from?
  2. Are bedrooms and bathrooms placed so doors do not clash?
  3. How large is the balcony, and does it open off the living room or a bedroom?
  4. Where is the kitchen, and is there room for a utility area?
