Serenia Kiwale project overview: House of Jets township in Pune

Everything about Serenia in Kiwale in one place: size, towers, homes, amenities and location.

2 min read 2026-11-26Serenia by House of Jets, Kiwale

Key takeaways

  • Serenia is a 10-acre township with 9 towers and 1,800 residences in Kiwale, near Mukai Chowk, Pune.
  • C Tower is the tallest tower in Kiwale and offers 2+2 and 3+3 Simplex homes on all floors.
  • 2 BHK from about ₹95 lakh and 3 BHK from about ₹1.35 crore, all-inclusive and indicative.
  • MahaRERA registration number: PM1260002602794.

Serenia by House of Jets is a residential township in Kiwale, Pune. It is also searched as Serenia Kiwale or Serenia Pune, and this page brings the main facts together.

Location

Serenia is at the Pune end of the Mumbai–Pune Expressway, close to Ravet, Akurdi and Dehu Road. Read the Kiwale locality overview for more on the area, and check live routes on Google Maps.

Amenities

The project lists 18 amenities, including a podium garden, swimming pool, clubhouse, gymnasium, yoga zone, amphitheatre, jogging track and a co-working lounge. See the Serenia amenities guide for the full list.
