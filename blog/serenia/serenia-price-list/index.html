Serenia Kiwale price list: 2 BHK and 3 BHK all-inclusive packages

Indicative all-inclusive packages for 2 BHK and 3 BHK at Serenia, Kiwale, with carpet area and parking.

2 min read 2026-11-26Serenia by House of Jets, Kiwale

Key takeaways

  • 2 BHK: carpet 674 to 678 sq ft, all-inclusive package about ₹95 lakh to ₹1 crore, 1 covered parking.
  • 3 BHK: carpet 956 to 962 sq ft, all-inclusive package about ₹1.35 to ₹1.40 crore, 2 covered parkings.
  • 2+2 (4 BHK) and 3+3 (6 BHK) Simplex homes are in C Tower; their pricing is shared by the relationship manager.
  • Prices are indicative and subject to change; confirm the latest price sheet in writing.

Serenia by House of Jets is a 10-acre township in Kiwale, near Mukai Chowk, Pune, with 9 towers and 1,800 residences. This page lists the indicative price of each home type so you can compare options before you visit.

Total area (including the balcony) is 746, 749 and 750 sq ft for the 2 BHK and 1,036, 1,038 and 1,042 sq ft for the 3 BHK. Carpet area is the usable area inside the home, so it is the best figure for comparing projects.

Simplex homes in C Tower

C Tower, the tallest tower in Kiwale, offers 2+2 (4 BHK) and 3+3 (6 BHK) Simplex homes on all floors. Pricing, carpet area, parking and floor plans for these are shared privately by the relationship manager. Read what a Simplex home is to see if it suits you.

What to ask before you book

  • Is the package all-inclusive of stamp duty, registration and GST?
  • Are maintenance, club and parking charges included or extra?
  • What is the payment schedule, and what does the EOI amount adjust against? See what EOI means.

Not sure which size to choose? Compare them in 2 BHK vs 3 BHK. Packages are indicative and subject to change. For a written price sheet use the enquiry form on the home page.
