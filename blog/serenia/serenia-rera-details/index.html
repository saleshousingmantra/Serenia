How to verify the registration

  1. Open the official MahaRERA website.
  2. Go to the registered projects search and enter the registration number PM1260002602794.
  3. Check the project name, the promoter, the address and the dates shown for the project.
  4. Match the registration number with the one on the agreement and the brochure.

Why it matters

A RERA-registered project has its approvals, timelines and details on public record. The registration page also shows the possession timeline declared for the project, so read it there and confirm it with the relationship manager in writing.
