Cookie Policy: Serenia Kiwale

What cookies and similar storage this website uses, why, and how to control them.

Updated 2026-11-28

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website saves in your browser. Similar technologies, such as session storage, work in much the same way. This page lists what this website actually uses.

What this website itself stores

  • No tracking cookies: this website does not set advertising or analytics cookies of its own.
  • Session storage (essential): when you arrive, the website remembers in your browser session where you came from (for example a search result or an advertisement) so that an enquiry you make a few pages later can be linked to it. It is deleted when you close the tab.

Third-party services

  • Enquiry form service: When you open any page, a script for the enquiry form is loaded from a content delivery network, and your enquiry is sent to a managed database service when you submit it. Those providers may receive your IP address and browser details.
  • Google Maps: map links open Google Maps on a new tab only when you click them. Google may then set its own cookies, under Google's policies.
  • Links you click: WhatsApp, MahaRERA and other sites you open have their own cookie practices.

How to control cookies

You can block or delete cookies and site storage in your browser settings. If you do, the website will still work, but the source of an enquiry may not be recorded. Because we do not set non-essential cookies ourselves, there is no cookie banner on this website.

If this changes

If we add analytics, advertising pixels or a chat widget, we will update this page and ask for your consent where the law requires it. See also our Privacy Policy.
