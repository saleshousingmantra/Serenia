How the EMI is worked out

The calculator uses the standard reducing-balance formula: EMI = P x r x (1 + r)^n / ((1 + r)^n - 1), where P is the loan amount, r is the monthly rate (yearly rate divided by 12 and by 100) and n is the number of monthly instalments. Banks may round differently, add processing fees or change the rate over time, so your bank's schedule is the one that counts.

How to use it

  • Start with the total payable from the cost sheet, not the base price.
  • Try 8%, 9% and 10% to see how a rate change affects the EMI.
  • Compare 15, 20 and 25 years: a longer tenure lowers the EMI but raises total interest.
