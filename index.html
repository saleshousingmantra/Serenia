FAQ

Serenia Kiwale: questions buyers ask

What is the price of a 2 BHK and 3 BHK at Serenia, Kiwale?

All-inclusive packages are around ₹95 lakh to ₹1 crore for a 2 BHK and ₹1.35 to ₹1.40 crore for a 3 BHK. Prices are indicative and subject to change.

What is a Simplex home?

A Simplex home is a two-level residence within one unit. C Tower offers 2+2 (4 BHK) and 3+3 (6 BHK) Simplex options on all floors. Read our guide: What is a Simplex home?.

What does EOI mean?

An Expression of Interest lets you register early for a preferred unit before formal booking. It is not a booking by itself. Read our guide: What does EOI mean for a new launch?.

How many covered parking spaces come with each home?

A 2 BHK includes 1 covered parking and a 3 BHK includes 2. Simplex parking details are shared by our relationship manager.

What are the unit sizes at Serenia?

2 BHK homes are 746, 749 and 750 sq ft total area (674 to 678 sq ft carpet). 3 BHK homes are 1,036, 1,038 and 1,042 sq ft total area (956 to 962 sq ft carpet). Total area includes the balcony. Compare layouts in our guide: 2 BHK vs 3 BHK: which is right for you.

Where is Serenia located?

Serenia is in Kiwale, near Mukai Chowk, Pune.

How large is the Serenia township?

Serenia spans 10 acres with 9 towers and 1,800 residences. C Tower is the tallest tower in Kiwale.

What amenities are planned?

Planned amenities include a landscaped podium garden, children's play area, swimming pool, clubhouse, gymnasium, yoga zone, amphitheatre, jogging track, games room, party hall and co-working lounge.

What is the RERA number and possession date?

Serenia is registered under MahaRERA No. PM1260002602794. For the latest possession timeline, please contact our relationship manager.

How can I book a site visit?

Call or WhatsApp us, or fill in the enquiry form and Rishabh will arrange your visit.
