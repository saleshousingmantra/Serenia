Privacy Policy: Serenia Kiwale

How this website collects, uses and protects the details you share when you enquire.

Updated 2026-11-27

Who runs this website

This website presents Serenia by House of Jets, a residential project at Kiwale, near Mukai Chowk, Pune. It is a marketing website that collects enquiries for the project's sales team. This policy explains what we collect when you use it.

What we collect

  • Details you give us: your name, mobile number, email address and the home type you are interested in, when you submit an enquiry form.
  • Where you came from: the page or campaign that brought you here (for example a search result or an advertisement), kept for the visit so that we know which enquiry came from where.
  • Technical data: like any website, our host and the services described below may receive your IP address, browser type and the pages you open.

How we use it

  • To call, message or email you about the project, prices, offers and site visits that you asked about.
  • To share your enquiry with the developer's sales team so that they can follow up.
  • To understand which pages and campaigns work, and to improve the website.

We do not sell your details. We share them only with the developer and with service providers who help us run the website and manage enquiries, and when the law requires it.

Where your details are stored

Enquiries are saved in a managed database service so that the sales team can see them. We keep them for as long as needed to follow up on your enquiry and to meet legal requirements, and then delete or anonymise them.

Your choices and rights

  • You can ask us to show, correct or delete the details we hold about you.
  • You can tell us to stop contacting you, and we will do so.
  • You can withdraw your consent at any time. This does not affect what was done before you withdrew it.

To make a request, use the enquiry form on the home page and mention your request and the mobile number you used.

Links to other websites

This website links to other sites, including Google Maps, WhatsApp and the MahaRERA website. They have their own privacy policies and we are not responsible for them.

Children

This website is for adults who are considering a home purchase. We do not knowingly collect details from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the website changes. The date at the top of the page shows when it was last updated. See also our Cookie Policy.
